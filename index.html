Our Story

A Greenville family business

◆

The Pace family opened the doors in 1948, and the business has been handed down through four generations since. A lot has changed in Greenville over those years. The way we work hasn't: honest advice, fair prices, and repairs done in-house by hands that have been doing them for decades.

1948

The doors open

The Pace family starts a jewelry business in Greenville, built on watch work, fine jewelry and knowing customers by name.

Pickens

A second shop on Main Street

For 42 years, a Pace Jewelers store served Pickens from its Main Street storefront, until longtime jeweler Riley Garrick retired and the family brought everything home to Greenville.

Today

Fourth generation, same counter

Now in the Village of West Greenville, just off downtown, Pace still does what it has always done: engagement rings, custom pieces, and watch and jewelry repair done right in the shop.

At the Bench

What we do

◆

Watch Repair

Any style, any age. From a quick battery to a full movement service, the work happens right here.

  • Batteries & gaskets
  • Link removal & band sizing
  • Spring bars, crystals & crowns
  • Timekeeping & service estimates

Ring Resizing

Sized up, sized down, or adjusted for comfort, with care taken around stones and engraving.

  • Up or down in size
  • Sizing beads & guards
  • Prong & stone checks
  • Shank repair

Custom & Bridal

Engagement rings, anniversary bands and pieces made for life's big days, designed with you across the counter.

  • Custom design
  • Remounting heirloom stones
  • Jewelry repair & restoration
  • Cleaning & inspection

Authorized for watches from Seiko, Bulova & Citizen.
