Most purchases in the store can be financed through Jewelers Reserve. There is more than one way to pay it off. Come in, and the staff will sit with you and help you decide whether a plan fits.

With Jewelers Reserve

Payments and the account are handled online. You can add another person to the account, at no extra cost, so a partner can buy a gift later in the year.
